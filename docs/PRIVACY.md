# BahiKhata Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: August 29, 2026  
**App Name**: BahiKhata (बहीखाता)  

---

## 1. 100% Local Device Storage Guarantee

BahiKhata is engineered as an **offline-first local application**. 

- **Zero Cloud Servers**: BahiKhata does not maintain remote servers, databases, analytics endpoints, or external user tracking APIs.
- **IndexedDB Storage**: All customer ledger entries, phone numbers, transaction amounts, notes, due dates, and app settings are stored locally on your device in your browser's / WebView's IndexedDB.
- **Zero Third-Party Tracking**: We do not collect telemetry, usage metrics, device IDs, or advertising identifiers.

---

## 2. WhatsApp Integration Privacy

When you use the **Remind via WhatsApp** feature:
1. BahiKhata constructs a standard `https://wa.me/...` deep-link URL on your device.
2. The message text is prepared locally using your selected language and template options.
3. The deep-link opens your official WhatsApp application. No data is transmitted to BahiKhata servers.

---

## 3. Data Export & Backup Privacy

You retain complete ownership of your ledger data. You can export your data at any time to a `.csv` file stored on your local file system, or clear all data locally using **Settings -> Data & Storage -> Clear All Data**.
